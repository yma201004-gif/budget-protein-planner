Data provenance and limitations

Generic AFCD records are joined by Public Food Key; skim milk uses per-100-mL data. Devondale light milk, rice, broccoli and bread use linked Coles product nutrition. Soy milk uses the Vitasoy manufacturer label; oil-blend tuna uses supplied packaging photos. Egg nutrition assumes 50 g edible cooked weight per egg. Barramundi uses generic raw-fish nutrition and assumes all 300 g is edible: bone removal is NOT verified. Pork packs are approximate; salmon has a unit price but no verified pack total. Rice, vegetables and bread add meal variety, not equivalent protein replacements.

Product data by WhichGrocer. Snapshot loaded through an authenticated MCP query, not an automatic website connection. Lowest portion price compares selected products only; it does not minimize the full-pack shopping total.
