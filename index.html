Data provenance and limitations

Generic AFCD records are joined by Public Food Key; skim milk uses per-100-mL data. Lite milk, rice, bread, salmon and oil-blend tuna use the selected retailer's product label; broccoli uses retailer-specific label values. Soy milk uses the Vitasoy manufacturer label. Egg nutrition assumes 50 g edible cooked weight per egg. Pork is sold at variable weight, so pack totals are unknown. Barramundi and turkey were replaced by paired own-brand lean beef stir-fry and lamb leg steak because no stable same-format store-brand pairs were available. Red lentils use the identical Katoomba 1 kg product at both stores. Rice, vegetables and bread add meal variety, not equivalent protein replacements.

Product data by WhichGrocer, queried 21 October 2026. Catalogue rows are snapshots, not an automatic website connection or live shelf prices. Lowest portion price compares the selected products only; it does not minimize the full-pack shopping total.
